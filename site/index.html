Fortis Wallet

Android · coming to Google Play

A non-custodial mobile wallet for Bitcoin BLAKE2b and Bitcoin Legacy. Your recovery phrase is generated on your device, encrypted under a password you choose, and never leaves it.

  • 12- or 24-word phrase with an optional BIP-39 passphrase
  • Send & receive with QR codes and a camera scanner
  • Choose your fee; tap a transaction to open it in a block explorer
  • Phrase and password screens blocked from screenshots
Coming to Google Play

How we build

Non-custodial by design

Keys are generated and stay on the user's device. We can't move funds, freeze accounts, or recover a lost phrase.

Standard, portable keys

A normal BIP-39 recovery phrase and BIP-84 derivation — your funds aren't locked to our app. Restore the same phrase in any compatible wallet.

Minimal data

No analytics, no ad SDKs, no third-party trackers. The backend keeps no account for you. See the privacy policy.

Our software is early. Back up your recovery phrase offline and start with small amounts.
